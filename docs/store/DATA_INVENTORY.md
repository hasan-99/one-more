# Data inventory and SDK privacy review

Verified against the code (`src/`), the migrations (`supabase/migrations/`) and the installed dependencies on 2026-11-18. Update this file, the Privacy Policy (`src/legal/legal-content.ts`), Apple App Privacy and Google Data Safety together whenever a data practice or dependency changes.

## 1. Data collected

| Data                                                                                 | Why                                                   | Stored where                                                                        | Who receives it                                                    | Retention                                                                      | Deleted by                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Email address                                                                        | Sign-in, account emails, optional notification emails | Supabase Auth (`auth.users`)                                                        | Supabase; Resend (only to send an email)                           | Until account deletion                                                         | `delete_my_account()` removes the Auth user |
| Password                                                                             | Sign-in                                               | Supabase Auth, salted hash only                                                     | Supabase                                                           | Until account deletion                                                         | Same                                        |
| Display name, shirt number, preferred position, skill level, strong foot, city, area | Player profile shown in games and teams               | `public.profiles`                                                                   | Other signed-in players who share a game or team; organisers       | Until account deletion                                                         | Cascade from the Auth user                  |
| Phone number                                                                         | Private contact detail                                | `public.profiles.phone` (readable only by the owner)                                | Nobody else                                                        | Until account deletion                                                         | Cascade                                     |
| Preferences (formats, availability days, radius, language, notification choices)     | Personalise discovery, send chosen notifications      | `user_preferences`, `notification_preferences`                                      | Supabase                                                           | Until account deletion                                                         | Cascade                                     |
| Games organised or joined, join requests, waitlist entries, reminders                | Core service                                          | `matches`, `match_participants`, `match_waitlist_*`, `match_reminders`              | Organiser and participants of that game                            | Until account deletion (games you joined stay for others without your profile) | Cascade (organised games are removed)       |
| Teams, invitations, challenges, requests                                             | Core service                                          | `teams`, `team_members`, `team_invites`, `team_challenges`, `challenge_requests`    | Team members, captains                                             | Until deletion; captaincy transfers or the team is disbanded                   | `delete_my_account()`                       |
| Chat messages and reports                                                            | Game chat and moderation                              | `match_messages`, `match_message_reports`                                           | Players and organiser of that game; support/moderators for reports | Until account deletion                                                         | Cascade                                     |
| Blocked players                                                                      | Safety                                                | `user_blocks`                                                                       | Nobody else                                                        | Until account deletion                                                         | Cascade                                     |
| Inbox notifications, email outbox and delivery status                                | Notify the user                                       | `inbox_notifications`, `notification_outbox`                                        | Resend receives the recipient address and a short template email   | Until account deletion                                                         | Cascade                                     |
| IP address, request metadata                                                         | Security, running the service                         | Supabase, Expo/EAS logs                                                             | Supabase, Expo                                                     | Provider log retention                                                         | Provider schedule                           |
| Session token                                                                        | Keep the user signed in                               | `expo-secure-store` (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore-backed); browser storage on web | Device only                                                        | Until sign-out                                                                 | Sign-out / deletion                         |
| Theme and language choice                                                            | Remember settings                                     | Device storage (AsyncStorage / localStorage)                                        | Device only                                                        | Until app data cleared                                                         | App uninstall                               |

Not collected: precise or coarse device location, contacts, camera, microphone, health, advertising identifier, device identifiers, browsing history, financial data. No analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDK is installed.

## 3. Deletion behaviour (identical in app, web page and policy)

`public.delete_my_account()` (migration `20261118090000_delete_my_account.sql`): transfers captaincy of teams that have another member, disbands teams with no other member together with their challenges and requests, removes challenges and requests the user created, then deletes the Auth user, which cascades to all remaining personal data. Provider backups, if any, follow the provider's schedule.
