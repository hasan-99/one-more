## Full description (English)
ONE MORE helps football players in Cyprus find a game tonight.

• Browse open games by city, area and format (5v5, 8v8, 11v11) without an account.
• Ask to join a game: the organiser approves players, and a waitlist offers places that open up.
• Organise your own game, with pitch status and open positions.
• Create or join a team, invite players and challenge other teams.
• Chat privately with the players in your game. Report any message and block players.
• English and Greek, light and dark themes.

ONE MORE does not book pitches or take payments: game fees are agreed between players.
